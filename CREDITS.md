# Credits

Minecraft Realism is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft, and it contains
no Minecraft assets. Everything not listed below (code, procedural textures, the synthesized sounds and music,
the default skin) was made for this project.

The built-in packs (`packs/default.zip`, `packs/music_*.zip`) are rebuilt with
`python3 -I tools/build_builtin_packs.py <downloads>`. Options > Resource Packs > "Built-in Assets" turns them off.

## Textures: Pixel Perfection Community Edition
- Block and item textures from **Pixel Perfection CE** (https://github.com/Athemis/PixelPerfectionCE), the
  community continuation of **XSSheep**'s Pixel Perfection, with contributions by StonePendant, freejusticehere,
  Stingraych, Nova_Wostra, lazerl0rd and others.
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  The textures are used unchanged, apart from the game's tint mask in the alpha channel of tinted blocks.
  They stay under CC BY-SA 4.0, inside `packs/default.zip`.
